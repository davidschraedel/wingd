# Wingd Dating App: MVP Outline

## MVP Description
The MVP is a referral-based dating app that makes word-of-mouth matchmaking a form of online dating. Instead of relying on an algorithm and endless swipes and social-media-like feeds, users can add friends, make introductions, and receive introductions, turning online dating into a more natural, social process, much like real life. The app acts as an avenue for users to be a wingman, or wingwoman, for their social circles, facilitating soft introductions and peer-driven connections.

## Main Goal
Centralize and digitize the word-of-mouth dating market.
