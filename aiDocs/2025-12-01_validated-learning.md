### What features am I testing?
Layout of the app, idea viability, discoverability/navigation, messaging, clarity.

### Hypotheses?
- If navigation labels are clear, users will find key pages within 5 seconds
- If messaging is clear, users will understand the main purpose of the product within 10 seconds of seeing the home screen
- Users will not understand all features, and will therefore report an average NPS < 6, CSAT < 4, and CES < 3.

### Outlined and collected measurable key success metrics:
- **Net Promoter Score (NPS)**  
  On a scale of 0-10, “How likely are you to recommend this to a friend?”
- **Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT)**  
  On a scale of 1-7, how satisfied are you with this experience?
- **Customer Effort Score (CES)**  
  On a scale of 1-5, how easy was it to use this app?
- **Task Success Rate**: average success rate of users completing a specific task
- **Time on Task**: How long it takes to complete a task
- **Task clarity score**: How clear is this page? Do you know what to do next?

### Documented your learnings based on key metrics:
I learned from our metrics:
- The app is easy to use, but visually more complex than necessary

- Users’ willingness to recommend the product varies (NPS)
- The experience is satisfying as an application but may lack some practicalities at this stage. (CSAT)
- The app is easy to use. (CES)
